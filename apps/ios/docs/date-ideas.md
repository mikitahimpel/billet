# Ourio — base date ideas catalog

150 ideas for the date deck and the randomizer (see [together-concept.md](together-concept.md), section 4.4). Card text is in English, the app's UI language. Aim: cute, cheap, doable this week for a couple aged 16–30. Nothing requires a car, alcohol or a big budget. Ideas with alcohol stay out of the base deck because part of the audience is under 18.

## Tags

Every idea carries four tags, and the randomizer filters on them.

| Tag | Values |
|---|---|
| Place | `home` · `city` · `nature` · `apart` (long distance, over video call) |
| Cost | `free` · `$` (under ~$20 for two) · `$$` (under ~$60 for two) |
| Time | `1h` · `evening` · `day` |
| Season | `any` · `spring` · `summer` · `autumn` · `winter` |

Card format: **Title** — one-line pitch, the text shown on the card. A short title fits a 2-line card, and the pitch stays under ~70 characters.

## Notes for implementation

- Store the catalog as a bundled JSON resource generated from this table, not hand-typed in Swift.
- Mix by place tag: 63 home, 48 city, 24 nature, 15 apart. By cost: 74 free, 61 `$`, 15 `$$`, so a "free only" filter still leaves half the deck.
- Season tags only lower an idea's weight out of season. They never hide it, because a couple in the southern hemisphere or a warm climate would lose half the deck.
- The weekly "new deck" from the concept can come later as small themed packs (Halloween, Valentine's, Summer) of 10–15 ideas each.
