The MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers billet's own code. The assets below are not covered by
it.

## Cats

The cats in `packages/pet-scenes/reality/Sources/PetSceneReality/Resources/Cats` (tabby, plush,
detailed, detailedKitten, detailedFluffy, chibi, kitten, tuxedo) are new models made for billet in
Blender. The detailed cats' fur, stripes, fur cards and normal maps are made in Blender from
billet's own model; no outside model or texture is used.

## Project assets

- `packages/pet-scenes/reality/Sources/PetSceneReality/Resources/*.usdz` come from the Nooki project.
- `packages/pet-scenes/rive/Sources/PetSceneRive/Resources/LittleHouse.riv` is billet's Little House
  scene.
- `packages/pet-scenes/rive/Sources/PetSceneRive/Resources/Rooftop.riv` is billet's Rooftop scene.
