# UI audit, 2026-11-11

Every screen, sheet and component of the app against one question: is it drawn by the kit, in the
lamplit-clay language of [cards-v2.md](cards-v2.md), with Apple's glass only on its chrome? The fix for what
is not is [one-system.md](one-system.md).

Taken at `175573cc` from the source (which kit component draws each surface), from `check-design.mjs` and
its baselines, and from the 84 gallery references in `packages/ios/design/Tests/Gallery/References`. The
gallery draws the components and three screens (Inbox, Who knows who, Draw & guess); the other screens have
no reference yet, so their rows below come from the code and the owner's screenshots of Shop, Inbox,
Calendar, Settings and Home.

## Verdict

The kit is not the problem; reach is. The package already has the slab (`cardSurface`), the section of rows
(`GroupedSection` + `Row`), the tile (`ItemCard`), the sheet (`BilletSheet`), the tab root (`TabPage`) and
the glass roles. Four things let a screen walk past it:

1. **A flat fill is legal.** `.cornerFill(.card, Ink.surface)`, `.background(Ink.surfaceRaised, in: .capsule)`
   and `.listRowBackground(Ink.surface)` pass every rule, and they are exactly the "flat dark rectangles"
   next to lamplit cards: 66 of them in 34 files. Nothing counts them.
2. **Three patterns have no component**, so each screen makes its own: the recessed slot inside a card (an art
   well, a collection's slot, an inset note: 9 places), the round control on content (a 44 pt circle, 12
   places) and a card that floats over a dimmed screen (a live `.shadow(radius: 24)`).
3. **A `List` keeps the system's rows.** The Inbox is an `insetGrouped` list with flat `Ink.surface` rows and
   grey system section headers on a page whose other sections are lamplit.
4. **Motion is unruled.** 116 raw curves (`.snappy`, `.spring(duration:)`, `.easeInOut`) in 43 files next to
   the `Motion` tokens; 13 files animate at rest (`repeatForever`, `TimelineView`).

its content sits on loose flat fills; **system** = Apple's own list or control where the kit has one;
**art** = a drawn canvas, exempt from the surface rules but not from the chrome rules. Lint = the file's
total in `packages/ios/design/lint/baseline.json`. Priority = visibility (3 a tab or a daily sheet, 2 a
weekly screen, 1 seen once or in Labs) × inconsistency (3 flat or system beside clay, 2 hand-made parts,
1 token debt only).

## Order of work

By priority, then by how much one change clears. Each line is one pull request.

1. **The kit closes the door** (unblocks everything): the well, the round control, the progress bar and the
   hairline in the package (a floating card needs no component: it is a card on a scrim); `flat-surface` and `raw-stroke` rules with a baseline.
2. **Shop**: Collections, Sets, Today's gift and bundle, Coins, the item sheet (rows 1, 2, 3, 9, 10).
3. **Inbox**: a lamplit section per day in place of the `List` (row 4).
4. **Us lists**: Wishlists, Date night, Watch together, Calendar (rows 5 to 8).
5. **Onboarding** (row 12), then **Mood, Games, Notes** (rows 14 to 16).
6. **Art screens' chrome**: Moments, the composer (rows 11, 13).
7. **Token debt** on the clay screens (rows 17 to 24), and the motion rule.
