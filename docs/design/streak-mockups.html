Love streak 2.0

The flame counts what the two of you do together, not app opens. A missed day can be won back. Same caramel evening, glass and honey button as the rest of Nooki.

A day counts when both show up

Each day the couple finishes one of today's flame tasks. A task needs both partners' part, so one person can't carry the streak alone.

  • Taps together: pet Mochi 30 times each
  • Today's question: both answer
  • Swap a moment: each sends a photo or a note
  • Do something together: one proposes, the other confirms

Half-lit

When one partner has done their part, the flame is half-lit. The other gets a push that names the task ("Masha did her 30 taps"). At 20:00 an unlit day sends one reminder to both, and the Us tab shows the hours left.

Winning a day back

A missed day can be repaired until midnight of the next day, in three ways:

  • Freeze: bought in the shop for 150 coins, the pair holds up to 2, used automatically on a missed day
  • Make up for it: finish 2 tasks together today, free, once every 30 days
  • Restore with coins: 300 from the pair wallet, either partner, any time

Streaks under 3 days can't be repaired.

Rewards

A lit day gives the pair +10 coins, a new flame color +50. Milestones stay 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365, each with a badge; the flame changes color at 7, 30, 100, 200 and 365.

Like TikTok's streak flame, the color says how long you've kept it. TikTok's tiers aren't documented: users report grey near the deadline, orange normally, purple from about 100 days. Ours steps at the badge days, so every new color is also a badge, and the whole app uses it: the Us chip, the card, the week strip, the widget and pushes.
